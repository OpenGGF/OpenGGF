section PLCs after the title card; the previous common sheet captured the
immediate-load tiles instead. The zone now keeps the common solid routine and
selects its sheet from the existing section-art owner. Its registry name is

all non-comment assembly statements are identical. Citation remapping found

needed for this comment-only change.
