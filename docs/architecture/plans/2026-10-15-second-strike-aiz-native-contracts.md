Missing art or either allocated propeller declines the optional pattern. Pending and already-created optional attacks retire when the recipe is withdrawn, the parent unloads or defeat begins.

Port selection is fixed throughout native descent/release. Knuckles declines the preset. Optional bomb fragments retain the exact ship identity through rewind and retire with the opted graph.
- Fire Breath's existing optional follow-up now requires ready native art and all three allocated warning actors before its timer is armed. A failed cue cannot become an unwarned attack when slots later return. Optional warning/flame state is captured and retires on recipe withdrawal/defeat/unload; ordinary attacks and six HP remain native.
