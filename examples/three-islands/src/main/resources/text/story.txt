# Three Islands story script. Original text for this mod.
# "@name" starts a scene. "Speaker: words" is dialogue; "> words" is narration.
# Scene names the game looks for:
#   prologue, <island>-arrive, village-<island>, <zone>-enter, <zone>-mid, <zone>-boss,
#   <zone>-clear, ending. Islands: south, west, angel, death_egg. Zones: ghz slz syz ehz cpz
#   mcz aiz hcz lbz dez. A missing scene is simply skipped.

@prologue
> Three islands. Three skies. Three adventures that were never meant to meet.
> South Island, where the Chaos Emeralds were first found.
> West Side Island, where a two-tailed fox builds aeroplanes in a workshop by the sea.
> And Angel Island, floating high above them all on the power of the Master Emerald.
> One night the stars went out of place, and rings of light tore open in every sky.
> Something was pulling the three islands together...

@south-arrive
> South Island. Dawn.
Sonic: Whoa... since when does the sky have cracks in it?
Flicky: Sonic! Sonic! You've got to help us!
Sonic: Easy, little guy. What's up?
Flicky: Badniks came out of those glowing rings! They're not like the old ones. They hit harder, and they glow!
Flicky: The shrine went quiet. Even the sea sounds wrong.
Sonic: Badniks, stolen emeralds and a broken sky. Sounds like a certain doctor's back in business.
Sonic: Tell everyone to sit tight. I'll handle this.

@west-arrive
