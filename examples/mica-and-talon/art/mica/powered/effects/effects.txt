# MICA strike trails and Gilded particles.
#
# trail <set> <ember-hot> <ember-mid> <ember-cool> <echo-tint|none> <density%>   (0xRRGGBB)
# front <set> <rgb> <radius> <size> <alpha> <arc>        heat front, outer haze first
# particle <name> <frame-ticks>, then per frame: frame <hotspot-x> <hotspot-y> and its pixel rows.
#   Pixels are hex indices on MICA's own live palette line ('.' is transparent), so Gilded
#   glints follow her live cycle (Gilded gold, or Hyper's Prism Gilt phases): 3 orange-gold, 4 gold,
#   5 yellow, 6 pale gold, c white. Slot 2 is the dark plate underside and is not used for light.
# emitter <set> <TRIGGER> <particle[,particle]> <count> <interval> <RING|SCATTER>
#   <offset-x> <offset-y> <radius> <speed-x> <speed-y> <jitter> <gravity> <life>
#   offsets in pixels, +x in front of her; speeds, jitter and gravity in 1/256 px per tick.
