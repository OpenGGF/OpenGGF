    About these numbers
    • Priority order can change as capacity becomes available; capacity and worktree ownership can permit backfilling. An available backfill allowance is not a promise that a job will fit or start next.
    • Estimates are coarse ordering hints, often based on class counts, not measured runtimes or start-time predictions.
    • Scheduling age includes carried waiting credit and is separate from actual wait. Unknown timings were not reported or could not be read.
    • Tooling revisions are self-reported; unknown may include versions from before revision reporting.
    • Observed resource checks use the current snapshot; only fresh wrapper reports explain scheduling decisions.
